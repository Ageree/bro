# bro — hero film cast

The landing stages `assets/hero-portrait.mp4` and `assets/hero-landscape.mp4`
the way [doji.com](https://www.doji.com/) stages its Mux cuts: one person,
dead-centre, on a light cyclorama, chrome as text. This is the footage.

## Photography bible (stolen from doji, not their models)

Pulled from their Mux frames (`Kf1Esm65wu01Gri…` portrait, `R5bEsJMnnw8G3jI3…`
landscape):

- Seamless light-grey cyclorama. No set, no furniture, no horizon line.
- Soft even studio light. A faint contact shadow under the feet, nothing else.
- Full body, centred, head-to-toe. Locked camera. Lookbook, not cinema.
- One figure at a time. They replace each other in the same spot.

What changes: doji's models stand still in fashion. Ours are people mid-text
with bro — they laugh, nod, raise an eyebrow, type with one thumb.

## How the film is built

1. Higgsfield GPT Image 2.5 stills, 9:16, white `#FFFFFF` cyclorama.
2. Seedance 2.5 `omni_reference`, 5s, 720p, silent: each still plus the
   doji.com portrait Mux cut as the camera / pacing ref. One person per clip.
3. ffmpeg `xfade=fade` 0.25s, order 1→2→…→9→1 so the loop closes.
4. Landscape is the portrait loop padded onto a `#FFFFFF` 1280×720 cyclorama
   (native 16:9 Higgsfield cuts came out rotated or pillarboxed).

Credits stay on the Higgsfield Plus seat; nothing from this run is committed
as a token.

## What was shot

Stills: Higgsfield GPT Image 2.5, `quality: high`, `resolution: 2k`, 9:16.
Clips: Seedance 2.5, `omni_reference`, 5s, 720p, 9:16, `generate_audio: false`.
Stitch: flatten each clip's cyclorama to `#FFFFFF` (edge flood-fill +
grabCut, so cream clothes and white sneakers stay), then ffmpeg concat
with hard cuts between ~3.2s trims, CRF 23, no audio. No crossfade.

Loop order: goth → babushka → looksmaxxer → old-money → worker → D&G →
suit → young man → redhead → goth.

Files:
- `assets/hero-portrait.mp4` — 720×1280, ~30s
- `assets/hero-landscape.mp4` — 1280×720, ~30s (the same portrait
  cuts padded onto a white 16:9 cyclorama)
